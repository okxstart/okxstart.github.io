just follow along

Start from any step you need. Just tap the green boxes in order.

OKX updates its layout now and then. The button names in this guide are the ones shown on screen, so follow those.

Security 1OKX05

  1. 1Right after sign-up you are at 2 / 5.
    Only phone and email are done.
  2. 2Authenticator app › Set up
    Write down the setup key it shows.
  3. 3Password › Add
    After the app, set a password too.
Changing the authenticator locks withdrawals for 24 hours

Setting it up right before a deposit is the worst order.
Finish this before sending money.

Security 3OKX07

  1. 1Open the app you just installed, tap +
    and scan this QR.
  2. 2If QR fails, enter the key manually.
    Copy key copies it.
  3. 3When the app shows 6 digits, tap Continue
    and type them in.
Never screenshot this QR or key

With this one value anyone can generate your codes.
Write it on paper and keep it separately.

    then paste it in the sending exchange.

Deposit 8OKX19

If Deposit account on the previous screen was Trading, you can skip this.

  1. 1Asset — pick the coin to move.
    If you deposited USDT, choose USDT.

Futures 1OKX20

Open
the futures screen

  1. 1Hover over Trade in the top menu.
  2. 2Click Futures to open the futures trading screen.
    The address starts with okx.com/trade-swap.
Don't mix it up with Spot

Spot is plain buying and selling, no leverage.
This guide uses the Futures screen.

Futures 2OKX21

Pick
a market

  1. 1Click BTCUSDT Perpetual at the top left
    to open the market list.
  2. 2Type the coin in Search crypto.
    Use the ticker: BTC, ETH, SOL.
  3. 3Click a row and the screen switches to it.
    Perpetual means a futures contract with no expiry.
Start with big markets like BTC or ETH

High volume means orders fill easily and prices jump less.

Futures 3OKX22

Switch
account mode

  1. 1At first the order button reads Switch account mode to trade.
    Press it — futures orders open only after this.
  2. 2Available at 0 means the Trading account is still empty.
    Move funds as in the last deposit card and it shows here.
A window asks you to pick an account mode

Choose Futures mode (on some screens: Spot and futures mode) and confirm. It's a one-time step, and it can't be changed while a position is open.

Futures 4OKX23

Set margin mode
and leverage

  1. 1Open enters a position, Close exits one.
  2. 2Keep margin mode on Isolated.
    It can't be changed once a position is open.
  3. 3This is the leverage. Tap to change it.
    3x on screen means 3 times.
Isolated vs Cross

Isolated: you can only lose what you put into this trade.
Cross: your whole balance is collateral — one bad move can wipe it all.
